NEW STYLE — "inked fresco": the drawing technique of 1930s hand-inked cel animation (as in the game Cuphead: a bold, even, dark brown-black ink contour around every shape, flat colour fills, one simple highlight shape per surface, clean readable silhouettes, effects drawn as solid inked shapes rather than photographic particles) applied to the subject matter and palette of Giovanni da Modena's Inferno fresco in Bologna. Grim and heavy — NOT cute, NOT rubber-hose, NOT a toy. Match the style of the attached already-redrawn shotgun sheet exactly. Palette: bone black, burnt umber, slate grey, yellow ochre, red ochre, dark madder red for blood, lime-white highlights; fire is ochre and dull vermilion; no neon, no glow haze, no glossy chrome.

BACKGROUND: the whole sheet background must be ONE flat pure magenta colour (#FF00FF) everywhere outside the drawings — no gradient, no shadow, no texture, no semi-transparent haze or soft glow spilling onto the background (every drawn shape has a hard inked edge). No grid lines, numbers, text or borders. Each frame stays fully inside its own cell with empty magenta space around it.
