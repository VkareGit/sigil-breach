Landscape 3:2 image. Paint a flat theatrical backdrop, a distant view of a burning ruined gothic city, as seen through the breach in the target painting: tall broken cathedral towers, one tower with a pointed window, a collapsing spire, broken walls and aqueduct arches, all as dark umber and black-brown silhouettes with ember-orange rim light and small fires at their feet.

The city rises from the bottom edge and fills the lower two thirds. Everything above the skyline and all gaps between towers must be perfectly flat solid #FF00FF with no gradient, no smoke and no glow on it. Keep the silhouettes crisp and hand painted, with no semi-transparent edges.
