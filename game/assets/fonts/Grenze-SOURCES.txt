Downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository on 2026-10-19.
The font binaries are unmodified; their local filenames omit the variable-axis suffix.
These files document RETIRED prototype fonts and their original licenses.
They are not glyph sources, fallbacks, or runtime dependencies of the active interface.
The old font-outline exporter has been replaced; no TTF or Font API is called by the
current importer or UI. Fixed line heights and baselines remain simple layout numbers.

Every current letter and punctuation mark is an independently illustrated image from
assets/art/alphabet-upper-v11.png, alphabet-lower-v11.png and alphabet-punctuation-v11.png
(the v08 illustrations repainted as fresco lettering; shapes unchanged).
The fourth source, assets/art/numerals-v11.png, supplies digits, percent and slash.
See assets/fonts/painted/README.txt and source-map.lua for exact provenance, crops,
component selectors and rebuilding instructions. Generated artwork provenance/prompts
are recorded in assets/art/prompts-v08-final.json.
